# ☕ Coffee Shop Sales & Business Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyses coffee shop sales data to understand **sales performance, customer purchasing behaviour, product demand, and revenue trends**.

The project uses **Python for data cleaning and exploratory data analysis (EDA)** and **MySQL for business-focused SQL analysis**.

The goal is to transform raw transaction data into meaningful insights that could support coffee shop managers in making better business decisions.

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The analysis focuses on answering questions such as:

- Which coffee products generate the most revenue?
- Which products are the most frequently purchased?
- What days of the week generate the highest sales?
- What time of day has the highest customer activity?
- How does revenue vary across different products?
- Which products contribute most to overall business performance?
- What purchasing patterns can be identified from the sales data?
